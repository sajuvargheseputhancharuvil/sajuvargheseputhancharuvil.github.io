INTRODUCTION

Investigation & Research Resources

Cybersecurity investigations rely on a combination of threat intelligence, malware analysis, digital forensics, OSINT, network analysis and security testing utilities.

This section highlights open-source and publicly accessible resources commonly used across SOC operations, DFIR, malware investigations, threat hunting and cybersecurity research.

Rather than focusing on enterprise-specific products, the goal is to showcase widely used tools and investigative platforms valuable for operational learning and analysis workflows.
