WHY THIS SITE EXISTS

Real Operational Learning
Looks Different.

A large amount of cybersecurity content focuses heavily on theory or simplified demonstrations that do not fully reflect operational environments.

Alerts can be noisy. Logs may be incomplete. Misconfigurations happen. Security tools behave differently depending on the environment, setup and available data.

I started documenting investigations, lab experiments, and operational workflows as a way to better understand how detections, security tools and monitoring environments behave in practice.
