FRAUD ANALYSIS

Cyber Fraud Administrator & Specialist Core Capabilities

Comprehensive expertise in identifying, analyzing, and mitigating cyber fraud threats targeting digital identities, web applications, and user accounts through behavioral intelligence, advanced analytics, and operational excellence.

1

Anti-Fraud Threat Topology & TTP Analysis

Account Takeover (ATO) Detection

Identifying unauthorized access via device fingerprinting, device ID tracking, global cross-application identifiers, concurrent parallel sessions, and abnormal network or geolocation shifts.

Malware & Remote Access Detection

Detecting banking Trojans, keyloggers, screen-sharing activities (e.g., AnyDesk, TeamViewer), accessibility service abuses, and automated overlay attacks.

Anonymization & Defense Evasion Monitoring

Tracking and flagging VPNs, proxies, hosting subnets, application cloning/sandboxing, Android emulators, and anti-detect browsers.

Authorized Push Payment (APP) & Social Engineering

Analyzing voice phishing/scams, public official/government impersonation schemes, fake rental/real-estate listing frauds, and romance/investment scams.

Mule Account Networks

Uncovering money laundering networks, multi-accounting behaviors, and family/group-based account pooling using graph analysis and non-transactional digital telemetry.

Advanced Spoofing & Attacks

Investigating SIM swapping (eSIM/IMSI changes), advanced GPS/location spoofing across iOS and Android channels, and automated card testing attacks.

2

Fraud Detection Systems & Rule Engineering

Custom Rule Development

Building, testing, and implementing complex logic rules combining behavioral metrics, session properties, risk scoring, and device attributes.

Rule Optimization & Performance Metrics

Measuring rule efficiency using precision metrics (Accuracy, Recall, Probability, Efficiency Score) to minimize false positives while maintaining high detection rates.

Blocklist & System List Management

Maintaining updated repositories for confirmed mule identities, compromised device IDs, high-risk geohashes, and hosting/VPN subnets based on verified incident data.

Integration & API Workflow Management

Configuring automated risk event streams (HTTP/Push APIs), debugging API integrations, analyzing log streams, and monitoring operational performance.

3

Operational Support & Investigation Workflows

First-Line (L1/L2) Support Orchestration

Establishing decision workflows for call centers and analysts to handle ATO, APP, and mule cases through client verification and session history analysis.

Incident Classification & Labeling

Systematically categorizing reviewed cases into operational outcomes for accurate reporting and threat intelligence:

  • fraud_by_fraudster — Unauthorized attacks
  • fraud_by_client — Socially engineered payments
  • mule_activity — Money laundering intermediaries
  • false_positive — Legitimate user anomalies

Graph & Entity Connection Analysis

Utilizing graph visualizers to map relationships between user identities, device fingerprints, IP addresses, geohashes, and shared payment corridors to uncover hidden connections and fraud networks.

4

Data Analysis & Investigative Tools

Jupyter Notebooks

Exploratory data analysis, custom scripting, and building investigation notebooks for fraud patterns and analytics.

ElasticSearch

High-speed search and analysis of large-scale log and event data for threat hunting and session analysis.

ClickHouse

High-performance analytics on large volumes of session telemetry and fraud events for deep-dive investigations.

Grafana

Real-time visualization of fraud metrics, operational dashboards, alerts, and trend analysis for monitoring and reporting.
